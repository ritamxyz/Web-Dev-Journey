ℹ️ About TechFest

TechFest 2026 is the annual technology festival organized by the School of Computer Science at Dholakpur Institute of Technology.

The festival brings together students interested in software development, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, robotics, cloud computing, electronics, and emerging technologies.

The event combines technical competitions, workshops, project demonstrations, expert talks, and student activities into one full day of technology-focused events.

Whether you are an experienced programmer or someone attending your first technology event, there is something to explore and learn.

🎤 Featured Speakers

Dr. Ananya Sen

Role: Principal Software Engineer

Company: CloudSphere Technologies

Session: Designing Scalable Web Applications

Dr. Ananya Sen has more than ten years of experience designing and developing large-scale web applications.

Her session will focus on how engineers make architectural decisions when applications grow from a few hundred users to millions of users.

Rahul Kapoor

Role: Cybersecurity Engineer

Company: SecureNet Labs

Session: Thinking Like an Attacker

Rahul Kapoor works in application security and penetration testing.

His session will introduce students to common web security weaknesses and explain how developers can design applications that are harder to attack.

Priya Nair

Role: Cloud Infrastructure Engineer

Company: DataGrid Systems

Session: From Localhost to the Cloud

Priya Nair specializes in cloud infrastructure and deployment automation.

Her session will explain how a project moves from a developer's laptop to a production environment.

❓ FAQ

Question 1

Is TechFest free for students?

Yes. There is no entry fee for currently enrolled students.

Question 2

Do I need to be a Computer Science student?

No. Students from other departments are welcome to attend.

Question 3

Can first-year students participate?

Yes. Some competitions have beginner-friendly categories.

Question 4

Will I receive a certificate?

Registered participants who attend the event will receive a digital participation certificate.

Question 5

Can I participate in multiple competitions?

Participants may register for multiple events as long as their schedules do not overlap.

Question 6

Can I register on the event day?

On-site registration may not be available if the event reaches maximum capacity.
