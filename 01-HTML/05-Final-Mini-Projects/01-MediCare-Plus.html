❓ FAQ

Question 1: How do I book an appointment?

Use the appointment form on this page and provide the requested information.


Question 2: Can I choose a specific doctor?

Yes. You can select your preferred doctor based on availability.


Question 3: Are video consultations available?

Yes. Selected departments provide video consultations.


Question 4: Can I cancel an appointment?

Appointments can be cancelled by contacting the appointment desk before the scheduled consultation.


Question 5: What should I bring to my appointment?

Bring a valid identification document and any relevant previous medical reports or prescriptions.


Question 6: Is the emergency department open at night?

Yes. Emergency services operate 24 hours a day.


📋 Appointment Policy

  1. Patients should arrive at least 15 minutes before their scheduled appointment.

  2. Appointment availability depends on doctor schedules.

  3. Consultation fees may change without prior notice.

  4. Patients should carry relevant medical documents.

  5. Online appointment requests are subject to confirmation by the hospital.

  6. Emergency cases are handled separately from routine appointments.
