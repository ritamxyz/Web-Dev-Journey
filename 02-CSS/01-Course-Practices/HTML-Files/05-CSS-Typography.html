Anthony Edward "Tony" Stark is an industrialist, genius inventor, and former CEO of Stark Industries. Originally a weapons manufacturer, an ambush in Afghanistan forced him to confront the deadly legacy of his technology, prompting him to forge a high-tech suit of armor to fight global threats.

As Iron Man, Stark became the cornerstone and primary financial patron of the Avengers, shaping the fate of the Marvel Cinematic Universe across more than a decade of storytelling.

1. Early Life & Origins

Inheriting Stark Industries from his father Howard Stark, Tony expanded the firm into a defense titan. After being captured by the terrorist cell known as the Ten Rings, Stark suffered shrapnel injuries to his chest, which he sustained using an experimental miniaturized arc reactor.

Alongside fellow captive Ho Yinsen, he secretly welded together the Mark I armor to escape, forever changing his worldview and turning him away from war profiteering.

2. The Avengers & Ultron

Stark helped defeat Loki during the 2012 invasion of New York, an event that left him with severe trauma and fears of cosmic invasion. These anxieties spurred the creation of Ultron, an artificial intelligence designed as a planetary defense shield that instead decided humanity required extinction.

Following the tragedy in Sokovia, Stark endorsed the Sokovia Accords to mandate United Nations supervision of superhumans, leading directly to a bitter rift with Captain America.

3. The Infinity War & Legacy

When the warlord Thanos decimated half of all galactic life, Stark initially withdrew into quiet family life with Pepper Potts and their daughter Morgan. However, the discovery of temporal navigation drew him back into the fight.

During the final battle for Earth, Stark channeled the power of all six Infinity Stones directly through his armor, sacrificing his own life to turn Thanos and his forces to dust.
